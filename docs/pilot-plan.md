# LuGRE snapshot allocation pilot

Decision: whether to test a lower-bit, longer recording for downlink of raw GNSS evidence. Compare GPS L1 C/A peak recovery across the same initial bytes/time of a fixed capture. First use an engineering positive control (OP2_0, PRNs 18/24 published by Parker). Then, if the archive is complete, pick the first L1 8-Msps, 4-bit-per-component snapshot of at least 240 ms in lunar orbit and the first on the surface based on the mission operation table, widening to all PRNs for these captures. Published detections and full-resolution results are cross-checks. The latter are internal comparators, not truth labels.

Compare the original 4I+4Q for 60 or 120 ms, 2I+2Q for twice as long, and low-pass filtered 4I+4Q at half sampling rate for twice as long. Also evaluate 2I+2Q for the original duration to expose the quantization cost, and zlib lossless size for the original data as a simple competitor. Count file format overhead in a future confirmation; this pilot counts payload bytes for analytical comparisons only. Quantizer cutoff is 0.98 standard deviations estimated from the first 10 ms per component, with levels ±1/±3. Use a fixed 10 ms coherent integration, noncoherent sums, and 100 Hz Doppler grid to screen candidate peaks. 100 Hz is a preliminary cost reduction and can produce a large off-grid loss with 10 ms integrations. No discovery claims or lock decisions follow from this pilot. Compute a peak/noise ratio and an idealized noise threshold with cell search correction. Compare same-frequency and code-delay peaks, then require independent PRN/Doppler confirmation and held-out tracking tests before making a design claim.

Practical rejection: if additional confirmations are absent beyond the best simple policy, or if an already published rule predicts the observed choice across held-out captures, the project has insufficient added value in its current formulation. High-confidence claim also requires replay of a suitable independent ground receiver, independently false-alarm-calibrated, and a packed recorder throughput test. Initial statistics are descriptive.
